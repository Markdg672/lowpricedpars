Condition guide

Pick the one that matches your club honestly. Photos help.

New

Never used, or used once. Looks new.

Like new

Light use. Very few marks. Works perfectly.

Average

Normal use. Some scuffs and wear. Works fine.

Poor

Heavy wear, scratches, or damage. Still works, but clearly worn.

Fair and protected — for both sides
