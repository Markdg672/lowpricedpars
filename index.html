Questions, answered straight

How fast do I get paid?

After we receive and inspect your club. Exact timing is coming from Carson.

What if my club arrives in worse condition?

We'll send a revised, lower offer. You can accept it, or get the club back.

What if my club is fake?

We pay $0 for fake, stolen, or wrong clubs. Photos help us flag it early, but the final check is in hand.

Can a photo prove a club is real?

No — not on its own. AI can flag something suspicious, but it can't certify a club. That's why we inspect before we pay.
