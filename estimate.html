Questions, answered straight

Can I trade in more than one club at a time?

Yes. Pick your models one by one and add every club to the same trade-in. There is no limit on how many you trade.

What besides clubs can I trade in?

Clubs, shafts, and other golf gear are accepted. If you're not sure about an item, put it in the sell form and ask.

Does condition affect the value?

Yes — it's the biggest factor. The better the condition, the higher the value. Use New, Average, or Below Average, and LPP grades each club in hand before paying.

What if my club arrives in worse condition?

We'll send a revised, lower offer. You can accept it, or get the club back.

What if my club is fake?

We pay $0 for fake, stolen, or wrong clubs. Photos help us flag it early, but the final check is in hand.

How do I get paid?

Choose Cash, PayPal, or Check. Your total updates instantly as you switch, so you can see exactly what each one pays before you go on.

How long does the whole trade take?

Shipping and inspection timing is coming soon from LPP. We pay after inspection — never before.
