What changes the price?

• Brand + model — newer, popular models pay more.
• Condition — the biggest factor. A mint club pays a lot more than a worn one.
• Completeness — full iron sets pay more than missing pieces.
• Authenticity — fake, altered, or stolen clubs pay $0.

How we protect Carson — and you: We don't pay before inspection. If your club arrives in worse condition than you said, we'll send a revised, lower offer. You can take it, or get the club back. If it's fake or the wrong club, we pay $0.
